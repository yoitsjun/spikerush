# The Spike reference images

Screenshots of *The Spike* / *The Spike Cross* (SUNCYAN) go here, for matching layout and feel only. They are never shipped: this folder is outside the Rojo tree, and image files in it are ignored by git because the repository is public and the screenshots are SUNCYAN's.

## Sources

The official store pages carry the current screenshots:

- Steam: https://store.steampowered.com/app/3983810/The_Spike_Cross/
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-spike-volleyball-story/id1510097347
- Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.daerisoft.thespikerm
- SUNCYAN: https://www.suncyan.com/

Gameplay videos (a paused frame is often clearer than a store shot) are on YouTube; search "The Spike Cross gameplay".

## What to capture

These are the pieces the game already imitates, from the 11 screenshots shared in the first session:

- the top bar: team names, VS, stamina bars
- the attack readout under the score ("129.75 km/h 3.85 m")
- spike trails (yellow, red and pink ribbons with lightning)
- the contact starburst and ring
- the white landing ellipse on the floor
- receive grades ("GOOD 71") and the shield on perfect receives
- "Free Ball!"
- the dotted set arc
- the blue triangle over the controlled player
- the "Team (Player) scored" banner with its reason tag
- the impact frame (white screen, red burst, black silhouette)
- speed lines
- the blue near-side net post

Still missing from the game, so worth capturing: the substitution and pause buttons, the skill icons, and the character select and upgrade screens.
