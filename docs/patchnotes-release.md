# 🏐 VOLLEYBALL RUSH IS OUT!
Spike Rush is now **Volleyball Rush**: a brand-new game and a fresh start for everyone.

## 🆕 New characters
**Horang** (S Wing Spiker): dig one of her spikes and a Tiger Claw marks you. Your next receive of hers fails. Her RAWR! on the first serve can Discourage the whole enemy team.
**Alexis** (S Middle Blocker, designed by a fan!): every block charges Fever. Press Q to boost your whole team. "Too cold there" can freeze an enemy spike right at the hand.

## 🏟️ Courts
- New Home arena, and every court has more going on: islands and a yacht at the Beach, a graveyard hill in the Haunted Forest, the next rooftop over at night, statues in the Colosseum
- Match point moments: a whale breaches at the Beach 🐋, the Headless Horseman rides through the Haunted Forest 🎃, fireworks over the Night Rooftop 🎆

## 🎥 Camera
- Settings > Camera: height, zoom and left/right sliders, with a live preview
- The camera zooms out when the ball flies off-screen
- Side seats removed so you can see those chase saves

## ⚙️ Quality of life
- **Max** button next to x1/x5/x10 for upgrades
- **Remove breakthrough**, one level at a time (fragments refunded)
- Menu outlines match your spike trail colour
- Ability icons on the roster cards
- New Plunge Spin icon
- Smoother ball: no more teleporting, and spikes dip like a real ball
